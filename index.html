Frequently asked questions

What is FlowDoctor?

A free static n8n workflow checker that reviews exported JSON for potential structural and configuration issues.

How do I check my n8n workflow?

Export your workflow as JSON from n8n, upload it or paste the JSON into FlowDoctor, then select Analyze workflow.

Is my workflow uploaded to a server?

The static analyzer processes workflow JSON in your browser. Account authentication uses Supabase. Optional AI beta sends reduced workflow structure to Supabase and Gemini after your consent. Avoid sharing sensitive workflow data.

Can FlowDoctor detect every n8n error?

No. Static checks can flag potential issues but cannot replace runtime testing or guarantee successful execution.

Is FlowDoctor free?

Basic analysis is free and requires no account. Pro is planned but not available for purchase yet.
